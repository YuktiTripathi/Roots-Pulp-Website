## Rules

- One primary phrase per page. Pages must not compete: implants, bridges and dentures each own their own
  phrase and cross-link for comparisons.
- Titles follow "Topic in Aliganj, Lucknow · Roots & Pulp" where it reads naturally (under ~60 characters).
- Anchor text varies naturally ("root canal treatment", "read about dental implants"); never repeat one
  exact-match anchor everywhere.
- New locality or "near me" pages are not created. Local relevance comes from the Google Business
  Profile, consistent NAP and genuinely useful content.
